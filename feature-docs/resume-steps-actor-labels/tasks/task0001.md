# Task Plan: task0001 — Actor labels for stop-recovery steps

## Goal

Every recovery step that `resume_conditions` and the stop report carry is
governed by one rule, owned by `batch-terminal-line.md`: it starts with an
actor label (AI-doable, or needs a human decision with one of three
reasons), and all AI steps come first. The develop skill's stop report
cites that rule for batch and interactive runs, the Result format example
shows a labelled value, and a new unittest module pins all of it.

## Requirements

FR1, FR2, FR3, FR4, FR5, FR6, FR7, NFR1, NFR2, NFR3, NFR4

## Scope

- `tests/test_resume_steps_actor_labels.py` — the pinning module for FR7

### Files to Modify

## Design

### 1. The rule in `batch-terminal-line.md` (FR1-FR4)

Placement: inside the existing `resume_conditions` bullet of
`## Field values`, after its current last sentence, as continuation
sentences, indented sub-bullets, or both. No new `## ` heading, no new
top-level bullet of any kind (a top-level bullet would end the bullet's
slice; one opening with a backticked key would also break the existing
bullet-order regex). The bullet's current sentences stay as they are and
contiguous, so every phrase NFR2 lists still matches.

Content the bullet states (English prose, Japanese fixed strings):
- **Labels (FR1)**: every recovery step begins with exactly one of two
  fixed labels, 「AI がやれる」 or 「人の判断が要る（理由: X）」, where X is
  exactly one of 「物理的に人しかできない」, 「戻せない変更の承認」,
  「価値判断」. The labels are these Japanese strings whatever the language
  of the step text (SPEC.md a6).
- **Human only for the three reasons (FR3)**: a step is a human step only
  when one of the three reasons applies; every other step is an AI step.
  Approval of a change that cannot be undone is a human step with reason
  「戻せない変更の承認」 (THREAT-MODEL.md TM-1).
- **Order (FR2)**: every AI step comes before every human step, without
  exception; no human step is followed by an AI step. AI work that can only
  run after a human decision is not its own step: it is written inside that
  human step as 「判断の後、AI が ... を実行する」 (THREAT-MODEL.md TM-2).
- **Step boundaries**: each step starts on its own line (in a batch result
  the line break survives as the existing `\n` escape, SPEC.md a5);
  alternatives a guidance joins with "or" are separate steps, each with its
  own label (a2); guidance that names no step carries no label (a3).

Wording constraints:
- Each of the fixed strings, and the fold-in phrase's fixed part
  「判断の後、AI が」, sits unbroken on one physical line, so a
  whitespace-normalizing matcher finds it.
- The new text adds no value-format rule and no rejection rule
  (IMPLEMENTATION.md D1). Nothing in `## Escaping`, `## Consumer
  constraints` or any other bullet changes.
- The document as a whole must still contain none of the retired phrases
  an existing guard forbids (case-insensitive): "terminal line",
  "four-field", "four fields", "key=value", "dual emission",
  "compatibility period".
- The scene-specific resume paragraphs (the write-rejection paragraph in
  `## Stop point coverage`) are not edited (IMPLEMENTATION.md D2).

### 2. The labelled Result format example (FR6)

Only the `resume_conditions` line of the fenced example changes. Its value:
- is one physical line; steps are joined by the `\n` escape, and any other
  character the value needs is escaped per `## Escaping`;
- holds at least two steps: the first an AI step, the last a human step
  whose label carries exactly one reason and whose text uses the fold-in
  phrase for the AI work after the decision;
- fits the example's existing stop (an implement step stuck on conflicts
  for task0004).

The example stays exactly eight lines with the eight keys in their order;
the other seven lines and all prose of `## Result format` are unchanged.
`## Escaping` stays byte-identical. In the same change,
`RESULT_FORMAT_TEXT` in `tests/test_batch_stop_contract.py` becomes the new
section text exactly; nothing else in that module changes (`ESCAPING_TEXT`
included).

### 3. The stop-report paragraph in `SKILL.md` (FR5, NFR3)

The heading, the opening batch paragraph, the three bullets (スタック /
中断 / YAML エラー) and the existing review-failure paragraph stay
byte-identical. One new plain (non-bullet) paragraph is added after the
three bullets, separated by blank lines; the recommended position is the
section's last paragraph, so it reads as covering every recovery step the
section names. It is in the file's Japanese voice and states:
- it cites the `resume_conditions` rule of `references/batch-terminal-line.md`
  (the path appears literally, and `resume_conditions` appears backticked);
- the recovery steps the stop report carries, including those the three
  templates above point to, take the same form (label first, AI steps
  first, a reason on every human step) in batch runs and in the
  interactive run's report alike (anchor words: 「対話実行」 and 「同じ形」).

The paragraph does not restate the label strings or the reason words, and
contains none of: a reason code, a sentinel value (including `no-step`),
any backticked field name other than `resume_conditions`, AskUserQuestion,
`decision: stop`, `needs_rework`, `classification`, `gate_id`,
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/batch-mode.md`, the Legacy
review-failure recovery label, or a `## ` heading.

### 4. The pinning module (FR7)

`tests/test_resume_steps_actor_labels.py`, following the conventions of
IMPLEMENTATION.md. Three extraction steps, each a pure function over text:
- **Bullet slice**: `## Field values` of `batch-terminal-line.md`, narrowed
  to the `resume_conditions` bullet, from its opening line to the next
  top-level bullet.
- **Paragraph locator**: the `SKILL.md` section from
  `## 停止時の報告（停止条件 2-4 のみ）` up to `## バッチ構造化結果`, split
  into blank-line-delimited blocks; the target block is a non-bullet block
  after the YAML エラー bullet that carries the anchors of Design 3.
- **Example decoder**: the fenced YAML block of `## Result format`; the
  `resume_conditions` line's quoted value, decoded per `## Escaping`
  (left to right, a decoded character is never re-read), then split on the
  decoded line feed into steps.

Matchers over those extractions:
- (a) the bullet carries the five fixed strings — 「AI がやれる」,
  「人の判断が要る」 and the three reason words — plus the human label's
  「（理由: 」 frame and the human-only-for-three-reasons statement;
- (b) the bullet carries the ordering statement (AI first, no exception,
  never AI after human) and the fold-in statement with 「判断の後、AI が」;
- (c) the paragraph cites `references/batch-terminal-line.md`, names
  `resume_conditions`, and states the interactive report takes the same
  form;
- (d) the decoded example: each step starts with one of the two labels,
  the first step is an AI step, no AI step follows a human step, each human
  label carries exactly one of the three reasons, and the example is eight
  lines with the eight keys in order.

## Test Notes

- All criteria are unit-level checks over raw file text; AC-7 is the
  integration run of the whole suite (it covers the existing pins in
  `tests/test_batch_stop_contract.py`,
  `tests/test_batch_quiet_output_skill_wiring.py` and
  `tests/test_review_gate_abort_skill_wiring.py`).
- Write the new module first, red against the current documents, then edit
  the documents until it is green.
- Negative-proof sources: capture the pre-change `resume_conditions`
  bullet, the pre-change `## Result format` example and the pre-change
  stop-report section verbatim from the base commit before editing.
  The pre-change stop-report section already names
  `references/batch-terminal-line.md` (in the review-failure paragraph) and
  says 「対話時と同じ内容」, so the paragraph matcher must need more than the
  path and more than 「同じ」 to pass.
- Matching: English anchors in `batch-terminal-line.md` are compared after
  collapsing whitespace runs; anchors in `SKILL.md`'s Japanese prose are
  compared with all whitespace removed (the file hard-wraps Japanese without
  a space at the break).
- Decoder edge cases: an escaped backslash followed by `n` is a backslash
  and an `n`, not a line feed; an escaped double quote inside the value
  does not end it.
- Existing pins the edits must respect, checked by the suite: the bullet
  phrases NFR2 lists; the eight-key bullet-order regex of `## Field values`;
  `CONTRACT_HEADINGS`; the retired-phrase guard over the whole contract
  document; the stop-report section's forbidden-literal checks (reason
  codes, `no-step`, all eight backticked field names together,
  `state=...` shapes), the single occurrence of the batch-mode reference in
  that section, the review-failure paragraph locator (first block holding
  the Legacy label), and the pre-change-prefix check of the section.
