# Task Plan: task0003 — phase-state 契約と互換規則

## Goal

Define the phase-state persistence format that makes create-spec and create-plan resumable, including its idempotency rules, exit-4 recovery procedures, resume decision table and legacy-feature compatibility rules.

## Requirements

FR13, FR32, NFR3

- `tests/test_phase_state_doc.py` — phase-state 契約ドキュメントの構造アサーション

### Files to Modify

(none)

## Design

Renders design-input.md 5.6 (5.6.1 through 5.6.4) and 5.12. The document states:

- The file layout under `feature-docs/{feature}/phase-state/`, one file per phase, committed to the integration branch, and the fact that `commit-docs.sh` already permits the path so no script change is needed.
- The full schema: top-level status values, `generation`, `base_revision` as informational only, `last_input_digest`, `active_request_id`, `packets`, `answers`, `worker_runs`, `artifacts`, `patches`, `progress_fingerprint`, `stale_redispatch_count`, `resolved_input_cache`, `last_error`.
- The rule that workflow.yaml carries no dialogue history.
- Identifier uniqueness and idempotency: re-appearance of an ID is a re-statement, and content divergence is a protocol error; re-answering a question is a protocol error rather than an overwrite, superseding requires a new question ID.
- The permitted `worker_runs[].status` transitions including the terminal `discarded_stale`, and the `active_request_id` lifecycle with its `discarded_stale` retention exception.
- `resolved_input_cache` semantics: cache is derived rather than recorded, so re-resolution may overwrite; reset on generation increase or re-initialization; `generation_digest` rather than HEAD as the invalidation signal; the three re-resolution triggers; the discovery caps and the `truncated` flag with its mode-specific handling.
- The commit procedure requiring `expected_base_tip`, and two distinct exit-4 recoveries: the phase-state recovery (refresh, re-read, idempotent upsert of the pending record, `base_revision` update, one retry, abort on the second), and the artifact-commit recovery (reset, then record `discarded_stale` and increment the counter in one commit, then recompute and re-dispatch, then reset the counter in a later separate commit), with the explicit prohibition on reordering the record after the re-dispatch and the reason (`commit-docs.sh` stages whole directories).
- The consecutive-retry limit of one with its state table.
- The resume decision table and the ordered reconcile reads, including that workflow.yaml wins when it is ahead.
- Size management: digests only, no worker output bodies.
- Legacy compatibility: the upstream-step status table for workflow.yaml without phase-state; the `project.design_system` backfill procedure and its placement between step selection and the `in_progress` update, with the reason; the loss-on-interrupt note for backfill answers; and the abort on an unknown `schema_version`.
