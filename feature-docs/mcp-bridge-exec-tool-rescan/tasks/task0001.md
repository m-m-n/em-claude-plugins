# Task Plan: task0001 — Limit the mcp-bridge skip (S1) to the notion / notion-create-comment pair

## Goal

destructive-guard skips the re-scan of the single-quoted `--args-json` value
of a static `mcp-bridge run` command only when the command carries the
`--mcp notion` / `--tool notion-create-comment` pair. Every other
`mcp-bridge run` command has its candidates re-scanned. The case suite pins
the new behaviour, and the in-code explanations state the pair condition.

## Requirements

## Scope

- `em-workflow/hooks/tests/destructive-guard-cases.json` — eleven new cases: TS-1 to TS-9 and two TM-2 cases

## Design

### Terms

- **S1**: the `mcp-bridge` arm of `_single_quote_skip_spans()`.
- **S2**: the `printf '%s\n'` arm of the same function.
- **Data word**: in S1, the word right after the word that equals
  `--args-json` exactly.
- **Statement words**: the words of the one `mcp-bridge run` statement that
  S1 already accepts.
- **Re-scan**: queueing a candidate that is not skipped to `pending`, so it is
  judged.

### S1 decision flow

1. The existing part of S1 stays: the second word is exactly `run`, and the
   data word is the word right after the word that equals `--args-json`
   exactly.
2. Side (a) is evaluated for flag `--mcp` with value `notion`, and side (b)
   for flag `--tool` with value `notion-create-comment`, by the side rule
   below. The positions of `--mcp`, `--tool` and `--args-json` among the
   words after `run` are not restricted, so the pair matches in any order.
3. Both sides hold: S1 returns the data word, as today.
4. Either side is false or undeterminable: S1 returns the empty set, and
   every candidate of the statement is re-scanned.

### Side rule (flag F, expected value V)

- **Occurrences of F**: every statement word after `run` whose value after
  shell quote removal equals F, or begins with F followed by `=`.
- A number of occurrences other than exactly one: undeterminable. This
  covers a repeated flag, a quoted flag word next to an unquoted one, and a
  `--flag=value` word.
- The single occurrence is not F written with no quote or escape character:
  undeterminable.
- The word right after the occurrence is missing, or is not V written as one
  contiguous run with no quote, escape or expansion character: the side is
  false. So `--mcp 'notion'` does not satisfy side (a).
- If the existing part of S1 admits a statement word after `run` whose value
  cannot be known statically (an expansion outside single quotes), that word
  makes both sides undeterminable.
- An abbreviated flag (a shorter prefix of F) is not an occurrence.

### What stays unchanged (FR2, FR3, NFR1)

- Conditions W1 to W4 and C1, the restriction to top-level chunks, the
  handling of ANSI-C quoted data words, and S2.
- The top-level span computation, the candidate marking by
  `_mark_substitutions()` and `_mark_quoted_substitutions()`, the extension
  of `all_subs` and the indices of its marks, and each token's `.unresolved`
  and `.substitution_only` flags. Only which candidates are queued to
  `pending` changes.
- The values of `scan_budget` and `MAX_SHELL_PAYLOAD_EXPANSIONS`.

### Explanation sites (FR5)

1. The docstring of `_single_quote_skip_spans()`, its S1 part.
2. The paragraph on single-quote candidates in the docstring of
   `scan_structure()`.
3. The docstring of `statements()`.
4. The comment in the queueing loop of `statements()` that carries the FR2

Each site states that the data-word skip applies only with the pair and that
forms whose pair cannot be determined are re-scanned. None states that the
skip applies to any static `mcp-bridge run --args-json`.
