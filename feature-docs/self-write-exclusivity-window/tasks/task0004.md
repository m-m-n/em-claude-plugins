## Goal

rework section 10 each show the return-time recheck between the comparison
(or the `validate-worker-output.py` validation) and the recording write, as
a citation of Rule R1, keeping their existing order and wording.

## Design

The shared terms ("return-time recheck", "recording write", the R1 citation
form) and decision D2 are in IMPLEMENTATION.md; they are not restated here.

Each site gets one inserted reference, in the paragraph (or list item) that
already carries `scripts/self-write-digest.py`, placed after that site's
comparison and its stale sentence and before the site's existing mention of
`Layer 5 recording`. The reference:

- names the `return-time recheck` (the Japanese site may add 再確認 next to
  the term) and cites `references/contracts/worker-envelope.md`, Rule R1,
  "Recomputation and comparison timing";
- states that it runs before the recording write;
- states that a mismatch, or a recheck that cannot run, makes the result
  stale;
- does not restate what the recheck compares, the membership matrix or the
  substitution, and does not name `git hash-object`;
- never states that the Layer 5 `Calculation` runs after the recheck.

Site-specific placement:

| Site | Existing anchors, in order | Inserted reference goes |

| develop SKILL.md design step 5 | `Scope verification` → `scripts/self-write-digest.py` → the compare/stale sentence → `Layer 5 recording` → `self_write_set` → `成果物` → `completed_at_commit` | after the compare/stale sentence, before `Layer 5 recording` |
| create-plan section 8 | `scope verification` → `scripts/self-write-digest.py` → `validate-worker-output.py` → the stale sentence → `Layer 5 recording` → `self_write_set` | after the stale sentence, before `Layer 5 recording` |
| rework section 10 | `scope verification` → `scripts/self-write-digest.py` → `step 3 validates` → the stale sentence → `self_write_set` / `Layer 5 recording` | after the stale sentence, before the sentence that records `self_write_set` |

**Constraints from existing tests (must keep passing unchanged,
`tests/test_self_write_digest_wiring.py`).** The tests use the first
position of each token in the item or paragraph, so the inserted text must
not mention, ahead of the existing mentions:

- in design step 5: `self_write_set`, `artifacts` together with
  `self_write_set` in one sentence, `成果物`, or `終了コード` together with
  `不一致` and `stale` in one sentence;
- in create-plan section 8: `Layer 5 recording`, `self_write_set`, or
  `exit` together with `stale` in one sentence;
- in rework section 10: `self_write_set` together with `Layer 5 recording`
  in one sentence, or `exit` together with `stale` in one sentence.

No site slice (design branch; create-plan sections 3, 4, 8, 9; rework
section 10) may contain `git hash-object`, "not a member", "member only
when", action-by-kind wording, "absent key", "is absent from", "holds the
dispatch-time value", or "dispatch 時の値に置き換え". The registered
sections keep citing `references/artifact-digest.md` and their
`Pre-dispatch verification` / `Layer 5 recording` section names
(`dispatch_digest_wiring`).
