# Task Plan: task0003 — artifact-digest.md "Layer 5 recording" When

## Goal

The "When" of `artifact-digest.md`'s "Layer 5 recording" reads as: after
layer 4 passes, the `Calculation` runs once R1's return-time comparison has
not made the result stale, and the recording write follows R1's
return-time recheck — by citation of R1, without restating it.

## Design

The shared terms ("return-time recheck", "recording write", the R1 citation
form) and decisions D2 and D5 are in IMPLEMENTATION.md; they are not
restated here.

The `When:` paragraph keeps its existing statement that the step follows
layer 4 (scope verification) passing for the dispatch's `completed` result,
and adds, citing `references/contracts/worker-envelope.md`, Rule R1,
"Recomputation and comparison timing":

1. `Calculation` runs only after R1's return-time comparison has not made
   the result stale.
2. The orchestrator makes the recording write (the `artifacts` entries)
   only after R1's `return-time recheck` has not made the result stale.

The paragraph does not restate what the recheck compares, does not name
`git hash-object`, and never implies that `Calculation` runs after the
recheck. The "Zero artifacts" paragraph, the "How" paragraph and its
bullets, the "Pre-dispatch verification" section (including its final
paragraph about the exclusivity assumption) and the "Dispatch sites" table
are unchanged.

**Constraints from existing tests (must keep passing unchanged):**
`tests/test_artifact_digest_docs.py` — the section still matches "after
layer 4 … passes", "one `Calculation` call", "every `written_artifacts`
path", the phase-state file names, "`sha256` the worker put in an entry is
never read", the `preserve` and `tokens.yaml` / `tokens.html` statements,
and "error element … fails layer 5"; `dispatch_digest_wiring` in
`tests/test_check_plugin_invariants.py` and
`tests/test_digest_contract_alignment.py` pass.
