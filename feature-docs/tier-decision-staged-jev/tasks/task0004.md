# Task Plan: task0004 — tier.yaml schema_version 2 and its projections

## Goal

phase-state.md defines tier.yaml schema_version 2, and the three projections of
that record (create-spec-phase.md's tier-decision record mapping table,
workflow-schema.md's `tier_decision.confidence` description, and the develop
skill's retrospect `signals.tier_decision` block) are aligned with it, with no
wording about two readings agreeing or disagreeing.

## Requirements

FR5, FR10, FR11, NFR2, NFR3, NFR6

## Scope

### Files to Create
- `tests/test_tier_record_schema_v2.py` — conformance tests for the record schema and its three projections

### Files to Modify
- `em-workflow/references/phase-state.md` — tier-decision persistence section moves to schema_version 2
- `em-workflow/references/phases/create-spec-phase.md` — Tier transcription section and its tier-decision record mapping table
- `em-workflow/references/workflow-schema.md` — tier section, description of the confidence sub-field
- `em-workflow/skills/develop/SKILL.md` — only the retrospect tier_decision signal block, per IMPLEMENTATION.md SC-6
- `tests/test_tier_decision_record.py` — assertions on the version 1 record shape or two-reading wording move to schema_version 2

## Design

The record's members and presence rules are IMPLEMENTATION.md SC-4, the score
object is SC-2, the `decided_by` distinction is SC-3, section ownership in
SKILL.md is SC-6, and resume compatibility is D7. They are not restated here.

### phase-state.md, tier-decision persistence section

- Defines every SC-4 member with its presence rule, the order of `bases`
  entries, the entry members, and that `pre_survey_estimate` is the Codex
  JSON verbatim.
- States that a schema_version 1 record remains valid on resume: reused
  as-is, not rewritten, not re-decided.
- Keeps the section's existing ownership and write-timing statements.

### create-spec-phase.md, Tier transcription section

- The mapping table has one row per SC-4 member, and one per `bases` entry
  member. Each row names its target: workflow.yaml `tier`, one of the four
  `tier_decision` sub-fields (`by`, `confidence`, `at`, `reductions`), a
  member of the retrospect tier signal, or an explicit "not transcribed".
- `tier_decision.confidence` receives one entry per `bases` entry: its
  `basis` plus the four bucket probabilities of its score.
- The table and the section say how a schema_version 1 record is still
  transcribed on resume (unchanged behavior).
- `readings_disagree` and every statement about the two readings agreeing or
  disagreeing are removed.

### workflow-schema.md, tier section

- `confidence` is described as the observed bucket `"0"` to `"3"`
  probabilities of each recorded basis, still not a single scalar.
- `tier_decision` keeps exactly four sub-fields; the section keeps citing the
  rule table and the create-spec-phase.md mapping table instead of restating
  them.
- No decimal threshold literal, no `P(0)` and no `expectation_clear` in the
  section (NFR2).

### SKILL.md, retrospect tier_decision signal block

- Members match the mapping table. `rationale` summarizes the final reading
  that decided the tier, or the fallback taken (from `fallback_reason`); it
  never states whether two readings agreed.
- No `readings_disagree` in the block.

### Review documents

- `em-workflow/references/review-phase.md` and
  `em-workflow/references/review-rules.yaml` are not touched (NFR6).
