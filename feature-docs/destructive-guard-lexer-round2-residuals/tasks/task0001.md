# Task Plan: task0001 — Command position after a closer `{` and after `time --`

## Goal

The lexer reads a `{` that directly follows the arithmetic-for header `))`
as the reserved word that opens a group, and keeps the command position
after `time --` / `time -p --`, so a `((1<<2))` in either place is an
arithmetic command and no line after it is skipped as here-document body
(findings 453963d025537b11 and 681fab61e1d9ff2c).

## Requirements

### FR1: `{` after a closer

- The set of reserved words recognized directly after a closer (the
  constant at destructive-guard.py:726, name unchanged) gains `{`.
- Behaviour: at a position where the after-closer state is set (directly
  after the arithmetic-for header `))`, after `fi` / `done` / `esac` / `}`
  at a command position, after a group `)`, after `]]`, after a `for` /
  `select` NAME), a complete word `{` is recognized and then handled exactly
  as a `{` at a command position: the next word is at a command position.
  This includes the adjacent form `)){`.
- What sets the after-closer state does not change. A `{` after closers
  where bash reports a syntax error (e.g. `fi {`) is also read as a
  reserved word; that reading removes, never adds, a here-document operator
  and is therefore on the fail-safe side.

### FR2: `time --` and `time -p --`

- Directly after `time`, or directly after `time -p`, the word `--` is
  accepted as the end of `time`'s options: the command position is kept,
  and the `time` option marker is cleared.
- After that `--`, neither `-p` nor `--` is an option any more; such a word
  is the command word of the timed command.
- The existing acceptance of `-p` directly after `time` is unchanged
  (including its current reading of a repeated `-p`).

### Edge forms to confirm on bash 5.3

`for x { ((1<<2)); }` and `select x { ((1<<2)); }`, each followed by the
destructive tail, with `rm` replaced by `echo` in the probe. A form bash
accepts and whose following line it executes becomes an additional deny
case and command-position form; the outcome for each form is stated in the
task report.

### Not touched

The arithmetic frame's close handling, the `lex_shell()` re-reading loop,
the settle channel, here-document operator reading and subscript reading
belong to other tasks (IMPLEMENTATION.md D2).
