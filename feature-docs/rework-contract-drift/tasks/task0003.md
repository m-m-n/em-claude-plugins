# Task Plan: task0003 — Required failure category: definition and verify-phase assignment

## Goal

The workflow schema defines a required category on every verify-step failing item,
drawn from a closed seven-value vocabulary, and the develop skill's verify step states
how the orchestrator assigns it — including the sentinel it assigns when the evidence
is insufficient, and that the verify phase never aborts on it.

## Requirements

FR3, NFR1, NFR2, NFR5

## Scope

### Files to Create
- `tests/test_failed_items_category.py` — pins the schema definition, the vocabulary, and the verify-step assignment procedure

### Files to Modify
- `em-workflow/references/workflow-schema.md` — the verify step's failing-items entry gains a required category field with its closed vocabulary, defined here as the owning site
- `em-workflow/skills/develop/SKILL.md` — the verify step states how the category is assigned at the moment failing items are recorded, and that verify never aborts on it

## Design

**The definition.** The verify step's failing-items list is currently a bare list with a
one-line comment. It becomes a structured entry whose category field is required and
non-empty on every entry, with the closed vocabulary given in IMPLEMENTATION.md Shared
Components: the review-perspective set plus a fail-closed sentinel. This document is
the vocabulary's single owner; the field's meaning, its required-ness and its permitted
values are written here and nowhere else. The definition states that a missing, empty
or out-of-vocabulary value is not interpreted as a default of any kind.

**The assignment procedure.** The develop skill's verify step gains the statement that
the orchestrator assigns the category at the moment it records a failing item, deriving
it from the failing verification scenario and from the requirement identifiers that
scenario maps to through the verification index. It assigns the sentinel whenever that
evidence is insufficient, unmapped, contradictory, or cannot exclude a security or
license concern.

**Where the abort is not.** The verify step states explicitly that it does not abort on
the sentinel or on any other value: it records the value and lets the case reach the
classification gate. Aborting during verify would recreate the gate-passage violation
the previous feature's specification forbids. The gate-side abort itself is written by
task0004 and is referred to here only by citation.

**Unchanged by this task.** The specification document, the verification document
format, the verification index, the retrospect phase and the rework planner are
explicitly outside this change and must be left byte-identical.

**Coverage.** A new test module pins: the field's presence and required-ness in the
owning document; the exact vocabulary, with a negative proof that an extra or missing
value is detected; the verify step's assignment rule including the sentinel condition;
the explicit statement that verify does not abort; and the absence of any restated
vocabulary in the develop skill, which must cite the owning document instead.
