## Design

The docstring describes the reading in prose, as it does today; it names no
variable or constant. The statements to add or change, by docstring rule:

1. **Rule 1 (command position and list state).** The state of each list
   stays of constant size: the three new states are values of its head state
   (M7). The open `{` groups are kept as records on one explicit list beside
   the stack, each with its command list and whether it is an anonymous
   function's body (M3). The words of anonymous arguments are not at a
   command position, and the `}` that closes an anonymous body gives no
   command position (M4, M5).
2. **Rule 2 (brace form).** The sentence that gives the next word a command
   position after a `}` that lowers the depth gains the exception of M4: when
   that `}` closes an anonymous body, the words after it are anonymous
   arguments.
3. **Rule 5 (compound-command closings).** A standalone `}` at a command
   position is a compound-command closing except when it closes an anonymous
   body (M4).
4. **Rule 6 (function heads).**
   - Right after the first name the reading does not change (FR2).
   - After the second name, every word other than a standalone `{` or `}`,
     an empty `()` and a separator is one more name, the reserved words, `!`,
     the head words, `case`, `esac`, `[[` and `always` included; the state
     and the command position are kept; a non-empty `(`, `<`, `>` and a
     comment `#` keep their reading (M1).
   - The three anonymous-function forms, the `() {` form at a command
     position with no head included, with blanks, continuation pairs and
     newlines allowed between the empty `()` and the `{` (M2).
   - The close of an anonymous body and the anonymous arguments, with the
     three outcomes of a standalone `}` among them, and the quoted, escaped
     and partial `}` that stay arguments (M4, M5).
   - The group-closing `}` as M6 defines it; the `}` that closes an anonymous
     body is no group-closing `}`.
5. **Rule 7 (head states).** The set gains the further-name, anonymous-head
   and anonymous-arguments states, with their lifetimes (M7), including the
   exceptions to "a separator ends any head state still pending" (the
   anonymous-head state survives a newline) and to "the next character rules
   it out" (the anonymous-arguments state survives words, redirections and
   closings inside a word).
6. **Rule 9 (re-read).** The re-read also restores the further-name,
   anonymous-head and anonymous-arguments states and the open `{` group
   records to their values at the `((` (M8).
7. **Stack paragraph.** The closing paragraph that names the lists running
   beside the stack names the open `{` group records as well.

Any other sentence of the docstring that M1 to M9 make false is corrected in
the same change. The docstring keeps every sentence that the existing markers
pin and that stays true.
