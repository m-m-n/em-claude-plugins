# Task Plan: task0002 — create-plan write_policy wiring: no fixed plan-document actions, existing task plans as targets

## Goal

Every create-plan dispatch passes the plan-document actions that the
orchestrator chose by `em-workflow/references/contracts/spec-writer-contract.md`,
"How the orchestrator chooses each target's action before dispatch".
`em-workflow/references/contracts/planner-contract.md` then names target paths
and their dispatch-specific constraints only and fixes no plan-document action.
The `replace_own` definition and the planner agent agree with that rule, and
the section 4 re-planning lists existing task plans as targets.

## Requirements

FR1, FR3, FR4, FR7, NFR1, NFR2, NFR3, NFR4

## Design

### D1. planner-contract.md names paths and constraints, not plan-document actions

The action of every ordinary plan document is chosen by the orchestrator by
`references/contracts/spec-writer-contract.md`, "How the orchestrator chooses
each target's action before dispatch". Each of the three places below cites
that path and section name and fixes no action for those documents.

- **"Additional input: `planning_inputs`" paragraph.** The planner's
  `write_policy.targets` cover `IMPLEMENTATION.md` and `VERIFICATION.md`, and
  `THREAT-MODEL.md` on the same terms (the existing re-plan authorization path
  applies unchanged). Each existing task plan under `tasks/` at dispatch time
  is also listed in `write_policy.targets`. A task plan that does not yet exist
  stays under `allowed_write_roots` (`tasks/`), with `written_artifacts`
  reporting each path created. The action of each of these targets (`create`
  for a path that does not exist, otherwise `replace_own` or the outcome of the
  digest-mismatch branch) is chosen by the cited rule. The minimal-tier
  `TASK.md` `extend_only` target and the `amend_other_feature` targets keep
  their fixed actions.
- **path_correction `write_policy` bullet.** `write_policy.targets` lists
  IMPLEMENTATION.md, VERIFICATION.md and each target's task plan. This
  contract fixes their paths only. Their actions (`replace_own`, or the
  outcome of the digest-mismatch branch) are chosen by the cited rule.
- **feature_docs_correction `write_policy` bullet.** `write_policy.targets`
  lists IMPLEMENTATION.md, VERIFICATION.md and each target's task plan, plus
  the `amend_other_feature` targets after a request
  (`## Other-feature documents` below). The plan-document actions are chosen
  by the cited rule, and an `amend_other_feature` target keeps that action.
  `allowed_write_roots` is empty.

Constraints:

- The phrases other tests pin stay present: in the `planning_inputs` section,
  "same terms", `create` and `replace_own`
  (`tests/test_threat_model_planner_contract.py`); in each correction bullet,
  `write_policy.targets`, `replace_own` and "IMPLEMENTATION.md, VERIFICATION.md
  and each target's task plan", and in the feature-docs bullet also
  `amend_other_feature` and "`allowed_write_roots` is empty"
  (`tests/test_other_feature_docs_planner_contract.py`,
  `tests/test_report_name_planner_docs.py`).
- The bullet labels of both subsections, their order and the heading
  structure are unchanged.
- No sentence of the three places says that a plan document "has action" a
  given value, and none names `phase-state/rework.yaml` or the last-entry
  rule.

### D2. create-plan-phase.md passes the chosen actions

- **Section 4, `write_policy` bullet.** It names the targets the planner gets,
  by citing the target set of `references/contracts/planner-contract.md`
  ("Additional input: `planning_inputs`"): IMPLEMENTATION.md, VERIFICATION.md
  and THREAT-MODEL.md, each existing task plan under `tasks/`, `tasks/` in
  `allowed_write_roots`, and at the `minimal` tier the TASK.md `extend_only`
  target. On re-planning after a spec-change transition or a tier upgrade,
  each existing task plan is therefore a target, so a task plan that
  rework-planner rewrote can get `replace_own` through the cited rule. The
  existing citation sentence of the action rule and the grant paragraph stay
  word for word. The bullet does not name `replace_own`.
- **Section 13, Pre-dispatch.** From planner-contract.md the correction takes
  the target paths; their actions are the ones chosen by the cited
  spec-writer-contract rule. The sentence saying that the actions named by
  planner-contract.md "do not apply here" is reworded, because that document
  no longer names them.
- **Section 13, Dispatch.** The dispatch carries the target paths of the
  `write_policy` of `references/contracts/planner-contract.md`, each with the
  action chosen in **Pre-dispatch.**, and `tasks/` in `allowed_write_roots`.
  The pinned phrases "`write_policy` of
  `references/contracts/planner-contract.md`" and "`tasks/` in
  `allowed_write_roots`" stay (`tests/test_final_path_correction_docs.py`).
- **Feature-docs correction dispatch.** The dispatch carries the target paths
  and the empty `allowed_write_roots` of the `write_policy` of
  `references/contracts/planner-contract.md`'s
  `### feature_docs_correction (dispatch-resolved value)`, each target with
  the action chosen above. No root is added to `allowed_write_roots`. The
  pinned phrase "the `write_policy` of
  `references/contracts/planner-contract.md`'s
  `### feature_docs_correction (dispatch-resolved value)`" stays
  (`tests/test_feature_docs_entry_develop_docs.py`).
- **All four places.** None contains `git hash-object`, a restatement marker
  that `tests/test_artifact_digest_docs.py` checks, `phase-state/rework.yaml`,
  the last-entry rule or a gate-id-shaped token. Section 13's six bold labels
  keep their order, and no new subsection is added.

### D3. The `replace_own` row (spec-writer-contract.md)

The row keeps its sentence "Overwrites the same-phase output the worker itself
produced earlier." word for word (`references/contracts/rework-planner-contract.md`
quotes it). It adds that, for an implementation-planner dispatch of
create-plan, this output also covers a planning artifact that the orchestrator
gave `replace_own` by "How the orchestrator chooses each target's action before
dispatch", rework-planner's recorded output included. The orchestrator is the
subject of the added sentence; no sentence tells the worker to compute or
compare a digest. The cell holds no `|`, and the table keeps its seven rows.

### D4. The planner agent (implementation-planner.md)

In "Feature-docs correction", the own-documents bullet states that the own
feature's IMPLEMENTATION.md or VERIFICATION.md is edited directly when its
`write_policy.targets` entry permits writing (`replace_own` or
`replace_authorized`). Under a `preserve` target it is not written, and the
planner returns `status: blocked` naming the path. The phrases
"IMPLEMENTATION.md or VERIFICATION.md", "edited directly" and `replace_own`
stay in one bullet (`tests/test_feature_docs_planner_rules_docs.py`), and the
other bullets of the section are unchanged.

### D5. The pin tests (tests/test_artifact_digest_docs.py)

New test classes are appended after the existing ones, under a banner comment
that names this feature and task0002. Existing test methods, and the module
constants and helpers they use, keep their behavior.
