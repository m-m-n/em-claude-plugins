# Task Plan: task0002 — Update the in-code explanations of --args-json handling

## Goal

The docstrings and comments of destructive-guard that describe how the
`--args-json` value of `mcp-bridge run` is handled state the new judgment:
the raw-string single-quote skip is unchanged, and every `mcp-bridge run`
statement other than the notion pair also has its `--args-json` value decoded
and inspected by `_mcp_bridge_args_json_chunks()`. The out-of-scope forms are
recorded. No executable statement changes.

## Requirements

FR8, FR10, NFR2, NFR3, NFR4

## Scope

### Files to Create

None.

### Files to Modify
- `em-workflow/hooks/destructive-guard.py` — the five explanation sites listed under Design; docstrings and comments only

## Design

The behaviour these sites describe is pinned in IMPLEMENTATION.md: the
component `_mcp_bridge_args_json_chunks()` and its contract, the constant
`MAX_ARGS_JSON_DEPTH`, decisions D1 to D3 and the out-of-scope list O1 / O2.
task0001 builds the component in parallel; this task describes it by that
name and contract and does not depend on its code.

### Explanation sites (FR10)

1. **The docstring of `_single_quote_skip_spans()`**, its S1 part and its
   closing paragraph. They keep stating that the skip names the raw-string
   candidates of the `--args-json` value only for the notion pair, in the
   top-level single static command (W1 to W4, C1, S1, S2). They add that,
   apart from this function, every `mcp-bridge run` statement in any chunk
   whose options do not determine the notion pair has its `--args-json` value
   decoded and inspected by `_mcp_bridge_args_json_chunks()` (D1, D2), so for
   the notion pair neither the raw candidates nor the decoded value is
   scanned.
2. **The single-quote candidate paragraph of the `scan_structure()`
   docstring**, the bullet on queueing. It adds that the span list and its
   queueing concern the raw text only, and that the decoded `--args-json`
   value of a `mcp-bridge run` statement other than the notion pair is
   inspected separately, in the statement loop of `statements()`, through
   `_mcp_bridge_args_json_chunks()`.
3. **The docstring of `statements()`**. It adds the chunks the inspection
   queues: the candidate bodies of every decoded string value (FR4) and the
   top-level `command` strings as shell payloads that take from the
   `MAX_SHELL_PAYLOAD_EXPANSIONS` counter (FR5); their origin position at the
   `--args-json` value word, in the paragraph on origin positions; that the
   rebuild, the decode and the walk are charged to the relative scan budget,
   and that nesting deeper than `MAX_ARGS_JSON_DEPTH` or a decoder failure
   other than a JSON syntax error takes the same early stop as the budget
   overrun (FR7, D3).
4. **The comment above the queueing loop of `statements()`** that carries the
   destructive-guard-false-deny FR2 label. It adds that the skip it describes
   concerns raw-string candidates only, and that the decoded `--args-json`
   value is inspected in the statement loop below whatever the skip names.
5. **The out-of-scope declaration in the docstring of
   `extract_shell_payload_anchored()`**. Its two existing forms stay as they
   are. It adds that the top-level `command` of a decoded `--args-json` value
   is another source of shell payloads, queued by `statements()` through
   `_mcp_bridge_args_json_chunks()` rather than extracted by this function and
   counted against the same `MAX_SHELL_PAYLOAD_EXPANSIONS`, and it names the
   out-of-scope forms of that source, O1 and O2, with the scope
   IMPLEMENTATION.md gives them.

No site states that the raw-string skip applies to a pair other than the
notion pair, that the decode applies to the notion pair, or that a form of O1
or O2 is detected.

### Phrases the existing unit tests pin

Some unit tests under `tests/` read these docstrings and require phrases in
them; for example, the `statements()` docstring must keep "origin position".
Every such phrase stays.
