# Task Plan: task0004 — Update the in-code explanations to the revised --args-json judgment

## Goal

The docstrings and comments of destructive-guard that describe how the
`--args-json` value of `mcp-bridge run` and the shell payload counter are
handled state the revised judgment: the words after `run` are read the way
mcp-bridge reads them, a decoded top-level `command` takes no payload count,
and the asks held for an exhausted bound or an unrestorable word are settled
after the queue is drained. The out-of-scope forms are recorded with the
reduced O2. No executable statement changes.

## Requirements

FR8, FR10, NFR2, NFR3, NFR4

## Scope

### Files to Create

None.

### Files to Modify
- `em-workflow/hooks/destructive-guard.py` — the six explanation sites listed under Design; docstrings and comments only

## Design

The behaviour these sites describe is pinned in IMPLEMENTATION.md: the
components `_mcp_bridge_args_json_chunks()`, `_queue_args_json_chunks()`,
`_DeferredAsks` and `ARGS_JSON_UNRESTORABLE_ASK` and their contracts,
decisions D1 to D7 and the out-of-scope list O1 / O2. task0003 builds or
revises those components in parallel; this task describes them by those names
and contracts and does not depend on their code.

### Explanation sites (FR10)

1. **The docstring of `_single_quote_skip_spans()`**, its S1 part and its
   closing paragraph. They keep stating that the skip names the raw-string
   candidates of the `--args-json` value only for the notion pair, in the
   top-level single static command (W1 to W4, C1, S1, S2), and that the
   canonical reading decides the pair. The closing paragraph states that every
   other `mcp-bridge run` statement in any chunk is read the way mcp-bridge
   reads its flags and has each static `--args-json` value decoded and
   inspected through `_queue_args_json_chunks()` and
   `_mcp_bridge_args_json_chunks()` (D1, D2), including the forms S1 lists as
   giving no data word.
2. **The single-quote candidate paragraph of the `scan_structure()`
   docstring**, the bullet on queueing. It keeps saying that the span list and
   its queueing concern the raw text only, and names
   `_queue_args_json_chunks()` as where the decoded value of a `mcp-bridge run`
   statement other than the notion pair is inspected.
3. **The docstring of `statements()`**:
   - the paragraph on the decoded `--args-json` value: the collaborator
     `_queue_args_json_chunks()` is called once per statement; every static
     value is inspected whatever the destination; the candidate bodies are
     queued like substitution bodies and the top-level `command` strings like
     shell payloads that take no count from `MAX_SHELL_PAYLOAD_EXPANSIONS`
     and are bounded by the relative scan budget (FR5, D7); the step's charge
     is added only when it fits, and on an Overrun or a charge that does not
     fit nothing of the statement is queued (FR7, D3);
   - the EARLY_STOP paragraph: besides the early stop of an ordinary overrun,
     a run that ends normally may hand over one held ask, settled by
     `_DeferredAsks` after the queue is drained: the scan-budget ask (the
     step's Overrun or a charge that does not fit, or a payload left unqueued
     by a used-up counter, FR12) before the `args-json-unrestorable` ask
     (FR11); with no list, that ask is emitted; a deny found in the scan still
     wins in main() (D6);
   - the paragraph on origin positions keeps the anchor of a decoded chunk at
     the `--args-json` value's word, which for the `=` form is the flag word.
4. **The comment above the queueing loop of `statements()`** that carries the
   destructive-guard-false-deny FR2 label. It keeps saying that the skip
   concerns raw-string candidates only, and names `_queue_args_json_chunks()`
   as where the decoded value is inspected, whatever the skip names.
5. **The out-of-scope declaration in the docstring of
   `extract_shell_payload_anchored()`**. Its two existing forms stay as they
   are. It states that the top-level `command` of a decoded `--args-json`
   value is another source of shell payloads, queued by statements() through
   `_queue_args_json_chunks()` rather than extracted by this function, and
   taking no count from `MAX_SHELL_PAYLOAD_EXPANSIONS`. It lists O1 and O2 of
   that source exactly as IMPLEMENTATION.md states them.
6. **The comment on `MAX_SHELL_PAYLOAD_EXPANSIONS`**. It adds that a payload
   left unqueued because the counter is used up holds the scan-budget ask
   settled after the queue is drained (FR12), and that a decoded top-level
   `command` is not counted (FR5).

No site states that the raw-string skip applies to a pair other than the
notion pair, that the decode applies to the notion pair, that a form of O1 or
O2 is detected, or that a decoded `command` takes a payload count. No site
lists as out of scope a form IMPLEMENTATION.md says is not out of scope.

### Phrases the existing unit tests pin
