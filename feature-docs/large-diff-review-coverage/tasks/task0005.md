# Task Plan: task0005 — Rescue-run findings, Phase R4 re-review shards and the Phase R5 coverage record

## Goal

Rescue-run missing-implementation findings never drive a fix, each Phase R4 loop
re-reviews per shard from the current diff, and the round record holds
`unreviewed_perspectives` and `partially_reviewed_perspectives` computed by the
coverage script.

## Design

Cross-task rules: IMPLEMENTATION.md D1 (run_id), D2 and D4 (shards and cap), D3
(passes), D5 (coverage), D8 (rescue runs), D9 (failure handling), D10 (this task
owns Phase R3b, R4 and R5), D11 (where the fields surface).

**Phase R3b, rescue-run findings.** A new paragraph next to "Reproduction
verification on orchestrator paths":
- Scope: in a pass where spec ran rescue runs, every finding of category `spec`
  whose `sources` include a spec reviewer identity is a rescue-run finding. A
  finding whose only source is the evaluator is out of scope.
- The orchestrator judges each one: a missing-implementation finding asserts
  that the implementation of a requirement is absent from the visible diff,
  rather than an error at a changed location. A finding it cannot decide counts
  as missing-implementation (A8).
- A missing-implementation finding is recorded `resolution: unresolved` with a
  `resolution_reason` beginning `unverified`. It is not an auto-fix candidate, is
  not counted in the residual critical/high count, and is not among the residual
  findings rework-planner receives.
- The same rule runs on the evaluator path, the evaluator-failure degradation
  and Phase R4 re-review output.
- It runs at the same point as the reproduction verification: after
  round-context suppression, before the candidate gate and the residual count.

**Phase R4.**
- The candidate-gate sentence that excludes `unverified` findings also names the
  rescue rule, and `file ∈ changed_files` refers to the round's full list, not a
  shard's.
- Loop termination: before each productive loop's re-review, the orchestrator
  runs review-shards again with the same `diff_base`, `changed_files` and
  `perspective_count` (the Phase R2 step, cited, not restated). It applies the
  cap again for that loop, and dispatches per shard with the Phase R2b rules per
  shard, spec order and rescue included. It records the pass: loop number, the
  files that loop's fix changed, and the manifest.
- The re-review run_ids use `loop{N}.{chain_index|fallback}` (D1). The pinned
  sentence "re-run ALL selected reviewers after any productive loop" stays.

**Phase R5.**
- Before writing the record, the orchestrator runs the `coverage` operation with
  every pass of the round and every `perspective_runs` row. A non-zero exit
  aborts before the record is written (D9).
- The Unreviewed-perspective disclosure lists every perspective dispatched this
  round with no completed run, whatever its last `skip_reason` (FR4), exactly as
  the coverage output gives it. Undispatched `requires_spec` perspectives and the
  axis 2 `source: tool` run are never listed.
- A new root field `partially_reviewed_perspectives` holds the partial entries:
  `perspective`, `unreviewed_files` and `reason`, with the D5 reason tokens. It is
  present and empty when there are none, disclosure only, and adds no gate.
- The YAML example shows both root fields as empty lists. They are placed
  before the `triage_filing` block, never inside or after it.
- The run_id comment in the YAML example states the D1 grammar, replacing
  `{perspective}#loop{N}`.
- The Completion gate paragraph is unchanged (`residual_critical_high == 0`), and
  the disclosure stays "disclosure, not a gate".
- The paragraph on `unverified` findings names rescue-run findings too: they are
  not counted, not sent to rework-planner, and not re-marked `deferred` by the
  batch rework-cap step.

**Pinned phrases to keep in Phase R5.** "List such perspectives under a
round-record-root `unreviewed_perspectives` field", "(present and empty when
there are none)", "This is disclosure, not a gate: the step still completes
whenever `residual_critical_high == 0`", "The committed round record
`reviews/round{N}.yaml`", "does not touch", "is the batch-visible channel", "`role`
field: `primary`", "whether that was decided at Phase R2 fan-out", "discovered
only after Phase R2b's chain walk exhausted every entry", and "R2b's" followed by
a line break and "malformed-result case". Phase R5 holds no backticked
`unreviewed` or `exhausted` token, no "gate_id:", and no mention of
batch-policies.yaml. In `tests/test_review_phase_llm_led.py`, only
`test_disclosure_gated_on_fallback_having_been_dispatched` changes, to pin the FR4
statement.
