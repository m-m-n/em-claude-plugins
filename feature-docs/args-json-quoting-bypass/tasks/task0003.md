# Task Plan: task0003 — `$` before line continuations and before non-ASCII in the word readers

## Goal

The word readers of the `mcp-bridge run --args-json` step read `$` the way
Bash does in two more cases: a `$` whose next character after one or more line
continuations starts an expansion makes the word Unrestorable and holds the
`args-json-unrestorable` ask (finding dc2fdf65ce30d198), and a `$` before a
non-ASCII character is a literal `$`, so the value is restored and inspected
(finding f35ed060564bcf20). The Unrestorable definition (predecessor FR11) is
amended in the code's docstrings and comment to name the new form.

## Design

Edit only the regions IMPLEMENTATION.md's ownership table gives this task.
`_read_static_word()` keeps its signature and its three outcomes
(IMPLEMENTATION.md Shared Components).

### One rule for `$`, shared by both readers

A private predicate (or pair of predicates) placed directly before
`_read_double_quoted()` decides whether a character can start an expansion
after `$`:

- inside double quotes: an ASCII letter, an ASCII digit, `_`, one of the
  special-parameter characters `@ * # ? - $ !`, `{`, `(` or `[`;
- outside quotes: the same set plus `'` and `"` (a `$'` or `$"` opener split
  by a line continuation);
- a non-ASCII character never starts an expansion (FR4).

Reading a `$` (both readers, same steps):

1. Skip any number of line continuations (a backslash immediately followed by
   a newline) directly after the `$`.
2. Look at the first character after them.
   - No character (end of body or word), or one that cannot start an
     expansion: the `$` is a literal `$` and the skipped continuations vanish.
   - One that can start an expansion, with no continuation skipped: the
     current behaviour (an expansion, so the word is not static).
   - One that can start an expansion, with at least one continuation skipped:
     the FR3 form.
3. A `$` with no line continuation after it is read exactly as today, except
   that a non-ASCII next character is now literal.

### Double-quoted body reader (FR3, FR4)

`_read_double_quoted()` applies the rule and reports one of three outcomes
over the whole body: the restored text; "holds an expansion"; "holds the FR3
form and no expansion". The whole body is read first, and an expansion
anywhere wins over the FR3 form. Its result shape may change; all its callers
are in this task:

- `_read_static_word()`, double-quote region: an expansion gives Not static
  (as today); the FR3 form marks the word Unrestorable and the reading goes
  on, with a quoted placeholder standing for the region's characters (never
  read as unquoted text), the way an undecodable ANSI-C region is handled
  today.
- `_read_static_word()`, locale-quote region: an expansion gives Not static;
  anything else marks Unrestorable (as today).
- `_read_word_as_written()` (masking stop): anything but restored text is
  undeterminable, the only safe outcome for it.

### Static word reader, unquoted `$` (FR3, FR4)

The unquoted-`$` branch of `_read_static_word()` applies the rule with the
outside-quotes set: the FR3 form marks the word Unrestorable and the reading
goes on (so a later Not-static element still makes the word Not static);
a non-ASCII next character makes the `$` literal. The lexer may already open a
region at a `$` whose `{`, `(`, `[`, `'` or `"` follows after line
continuations; a region kind other than a quote keeps giving Not static (FR3:
a word holding another FR6 (1) element stays Not static). The tests below pin
the outcome of each sequence whichever path produces it.

### Docstrings and comment (FR7, FR10)

The FR3 form is written everywhere as: a `$`, one or more line continuations
(a backslash and a newline), and a character that starts an expansion.

- `_read_static_word()` docstring: the WORD_UNRESTORABLE paragraph names the
  FR3 form; the WORD_STATIC / WORD_NOT_STATIC paragraphs say a `$` before a
  non-ASCII character is literal.
- `_read_double_quoted()` docstring: the line-continuation rule, the
  non-ASCII rule and the three outcomes.
- `_read_word_as_written()` docstring: the double-quoted FR3 form is
  undeterminable.
- `_mcp_bridge_args_json_chunks()` docstring, step 4: the Unrestorable list
  names the FR3 form.
- Comment above `ARGS_JSON_UNRESTORABLE_ASK`: names the FR3 form.
- The reason id `args-json-unrestorable` and the reason text do not change.
