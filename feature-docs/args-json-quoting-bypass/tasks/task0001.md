when the `--format`, `--session` and `--args-json` value words are

### Single-word-certain helper (new, FR1, NFR6)

- Contract: takes the marked text and one word; true exactly when Bash always
  passes that word as one argument; false for everything else, including
  anything the helper cannot settle (no span, no marked text, marker residue,
  a quote region the word does not hold whole, an unclosed region).
- It reads the word's as-written characters and the lexer's top-level region
  kinds over them, the way the existing readers do, and calls none of
  `_read_double_quoted()`, `_read_static_word()`, `_read_word_as_written()`
  (IMPLEMENTATION.md D2).
- A word is single-word-certain when, left to right:
  - a single-quote region, an ANSI-C quote region (decodable or not) and plain
    unquoted literal characters (backslash escapes included) count as certain;
  - a double-quote region counts as certain unless its content holds an
    `@`-form array expansion: `$@`, or a `${...}` whose content holds `@`
    (covers `${name[@]...}` and `${!prefix@}`). A command substitution or a
    parameter expansion inside the double quotes (`"$(cat comment.json)"`,
    `"$SID"`) is certain. A locale quote region gets the same check as a
    double-quote region (safe side);
  - any other top-level region kind (command substitution, parameter or
    arithmetic expansion, subscript, process substitution) makes the word not
    certain;
  - an unquoted `$` that is not the opener of an ANSI-C or locale quote, and
    an unquoted backtick, make the word not certain;
  - an unquoted brace pair, an unquoted glob character, an unquoted bracket
    pair, an extended-glob form and a leading unquoted tilde make the word not
    certain (the same forms `_read_static_word()` rejects as unquoted globs,
    braces and tildes).
- It is the only place the judgment is made. `_single_quote_skip_spans()`
  does not change.

### Notion-pair predicate (FR1)

`_args_json_notion_pair()` keeps every current condition and adds one: every
value word of `--format`, `--session` and `--args-json` (all occurrences) is
single-word-certain. A statement that fails it goes on to the existing flag
reading unchanged. The `--mcp` / `--tool` checks stay as they are.

Expected effect on the scenarios: TS-1 to TS-4 are no longer excluded; the
flag reading takes the non-certain value word as the flag's value and reads on
to the static `--args-json` value, which is decoded and gives deny. TS-5
(`--session "$SID"`) stays excluded and allow.

  ANSI-C forms are asserted at the predicate too: KIND_NOTHING alone cannot
  show they are still excluded.

  of `test_o2_forms`: a few phrases that carry the scope, asserted in the
  "Out of scope" part of the decision's docstring and in the
