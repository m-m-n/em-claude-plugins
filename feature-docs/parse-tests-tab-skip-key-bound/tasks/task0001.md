# Task Plan: task0001 — Bound the pre-first-item continuation skip in parse_tests by the tests key depth

## Goal

In the record checker's `_RecordParser.parse_tests`, the pre-first-item scan passes over only the lines deeper than both the preceding tab line and the `tests` key after it reports a continuation line, so a second `tests` key or a dash item at the key's own depth is read and reported again; regression tests pin this.

## Requirements

FR1, FR2, FR3, FR4, NFR1, NFR2, NFR3, NFR4

## Scope

### Files to Create

None.

### Files to Modify

- `tests/test_tests_yaml_id_resolution.py` — change the skip depth bound in the pre-first-item scan of the parse_tests method, update its docstring and the scan comment, and add regression tests to the TestTabLinesInTestsBlock class

## Design

### Where

`_RecordParser.parse_tests` in `tests/test_tests_yaml_id_resolution.py`: the scan that runs before the first item of a `tests` block (the "pre-first-item scan"). Approximate locations at planning time: the skip call near line 644, the continuation-line decision near lines 637-642, the method docstring near lines 586-589, the scan comment near lines 617-619. Line numbers are hints; locate by content.

### Current behavior (defect)

When the pre-first-item scan meets a line that has a tab in its indentation (a "tab line"), and a following non-dash line is deeper than both the `tests` key and that tab line, the scan reports that line as a continuation line and then passes over every following line deeper than the tab line alone. When the tab line is shallower than the `tests` key (for example key at 4 spaces, tab line at 3 spaces), lines at exactly the key's depth — a second `tests` key, or a dash item at the key's depth — are deeper than the tab line and are therefore passed over silently: their IDs are not extracted and errors such as `duplicate tests key` are not reported.

### Required behavior (FR1, FR2)

- After reporting a continuation line, the scan passes over only lines strictly deeper than the greater of two depths: the preceding tab line's depth and the `tests` key's depth.
- The first following line at or shallower than that greater depth ends the pass-over, and scanning resumes at that line exactly as it would for any line reached by the scan (it is not consumed or reported by the pass-over).
- When the tab line is at or deeper than the `tests` key (FR2), the greater depth equals the tab line's depth, so behavior is identical to the current behavior.
- Unchanged: the continuation-line decision itself (a tab line has been seen, the line is not a dash, the line is deeper than the `tests` key, the line is deeper than the tab line); all error wording, including `tab in indentation` and `tests item continues or nests on a deeper line (multi-line or nested value)`; the contract of the existing pass-over helper (only the depth bound the pre-first-item scan supplies to it changes); every other part of the parser.

### Resumption flow when the resumed line is a dash item at the key's depth

1. The resumed dash line starts the item block; the item depth is taken from that line (equal to the `tests` key's depth).
2. The item loop ends at the next non-dash line at that same depth (for example a second `tests` key).
3. The field loop then reads that line as a key; a second `tests` key yields the `duplicate tests key` error and its IDs are extracted.

### Docstring and comment (FR3)

Both the `parse_tests` docstring and the pre-first-item scan comment currently say that the lines deeper than the tab line are passed over. Reword both so they say that the lines deeper than both the tab line and the `tests` key are passed over. No other docstring or comment changes are required.

### Test records

In every record below, lines are numbered from 1, indentation is spaces unless a tab is stated, and every tab is written in the test source as an escape sequence inside a string literal, following the existing record helper `_tab_record` (NFR3).
