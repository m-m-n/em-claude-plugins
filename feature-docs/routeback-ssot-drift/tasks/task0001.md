### A. `workflow-schema.md` agents.jsonl paragraph

Required content after the edit. Wording is free except where marked
"verbatim".

1. **Readers (FR1)** — the paragraph names every reader `implement-phase.md`
   defines:
   - the stop-tool recorder, `queue_taskstop_net.py`, at stop;
   - the SubagentStop failure net, `queue_failure_net.py`, through its
     agent-index fallback (used only when no assignment block is found);
   - the orchestrator itself, in I.2.b step 1's recovery check, through
     `implement-phase.md`'s Orchestrator-side read, including the
     orphan-recovery evidence chain.
   It cites `implement-phase.md` for these rules instead of restating them.
   The writer statement (`queue_agent_index.py` at launch) stays. The base
   sentence form that ends right after "read by `queue_taskstop_net.py` at
   stop." (the sole-reader form) no longer appears.
2. **Purpose (FR2)** — the index resolves a stop or a recovery check back to
   a task and its worktree. The phrase "exists solely to make a stop
   resolvable back to a task" no longer appears anywhere in the file.
3. **Absence (FR3)** — an absent or stale index does more than turn the
   stop-tool recorder into a no-op: it also makes the Orchestrator-side
   lookup unresolvable, which leads to Residual (journal unchanged, task
   stays in-flight, route-back gate blocks, gate-rejected terminal); and
   when the index has no entry for the task, orphan recovery stops at
   `no-agent-entry`. Both points are stated by citing `implement-phase.md`'s
   I.2.b Recovery / Residual block. The phrase "its absence only degrades
   the stop-tool recorder to a no-op" no longer appears anywhere in the
   file.
4. **Two read rules (FR4)** — the paragraph names both rules with their
   owners and states that their "ambiguous" conditions are different
   conditions:
   - stop-side resolution — owners `queue_taskstop_net.py` and
     IMPLEMENTATION.md's Agent index contract (the failure net's fallback
     resolves with the same rule); "ambiguous" means the identifier matches
     entries of two or more distinct (agents.jsonl, task) pairs;
   - orchestrator-side read — owner `implement-phase.md`'s Orchestrator-side
     read; "ambiguous" means the selected entry names no usable candidate.
5. **Retained verbatim (NFR2)** — existing tests pin these (compared with
   whitespace normalized); each must survive exactly:
   - "it carries no status semantics of its own, may be absent or stale"
   - "The sole exception to "no status semantics of its own" is the session
     identity"
   - "em-workflow/references/implement-phase.md`'s I.2.b Recovery / Residual
     block" (the citation form, including the backtick placement)
   - the opening clause "The same worktree-root directory also holds
     `agents.jsonl`, the agent index" (the slicing anchor for this task's
     tests).
   The statements that the index is not part of the journal contract, is
   never a second authoritative state file, and that `journal.jsonl` alone
   is the authoritative raw-event record also stay (reworded only if
   needed).
6. **Prohibited** — the token `stale-agent-entry` must not appear anywhere
   in `workflow-schema.md` (an existing test asserts its absence).
