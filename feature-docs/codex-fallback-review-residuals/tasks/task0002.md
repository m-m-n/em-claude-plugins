## Design

The agent name is fixed in IMPLEMENTATION.md (Shared Components, D1). This task reads and edits README text only.

### 1. The `--batch` bullet

The bullet is the single line that begins 「`--batch` で無人実行モードになる」. Its opening text and every clause other than the one below stay as they are, and the bullet stays one line.

The clause to replace is the tail of the unlisted-gate sentence: 「…に従うが、仕様変更・セキュリティ・ライセンス・不可逆判断のゲートは未収載なら安全側で中断する（fail-closed）。」 The replacement, in Japanese and in the register of the surrounding text, states:

1. Interactive mode: security, license and irreversible-operation questions abort.
2. Batch mode: the same questions take the relaxed route — Codex 相談 → Opus escalation → 最小副作用の選択肢 — and do not abort.
3. Where the conditions live: `references/question-resolution.md`, naming its "The batch relaxation" and "The surviving aborts" parts, with no trigger condition restated.
4. One sentence: the surviving aborts named there are the only fail-closed classification stops left in batch.

The first half of the unlisted-gate sentence (its fallback description) is untouched.
