## Design

### What the revised I.2.a must say

One rule, stated once, rendering exactly the Hook classification contract in IMPLEMENTATION.md Shared Components:

1. The recycled-task-id rule applies to the orchestrator's interpretation of the journal AND to `queue_stop_guard.py`, which applies the same carve-out itself.
2. `queue_launch_guard.py`, `queue_failure_net.py` and `queue_taskstop_net.py` derive a task's state from the journal's last event alone and never consult `tasks.{T}.status`.

The removed literal is `governs only the orchestrator's interpretation of the journal` — it is exactly the clause the following exception contradicts, so it must not survive anywhere in the document. The weaker, TRUE claim about workflow.yaml reads is preserved by keeping the document free of both `never read workflow.yaml` and `never reads workflow.yaml`. The journal's append-only nature and the "only the interpretation of its events is scoped by this rule" statement stay.

### The divergence statement (FR6)

Appended to the same paragraph, marked as deliberate and as intended hook behavior rather than a defect: I.2.a defines unlaunched as "no journal event yet AND status != merged", whereas the Stop hook classifies a task with NO journal event as unlaunched without consulting its workflow.yaml status at all — so on a missing or truncated journal, a task whose workflow.yaml status reads `merged` can appear in the hook's launch list. The recorded reason is that the hook is a fail-open net, not an authority. No hook file is touched by this task.

### The Supporting-cast bullet (FR2, NFR6)

The bullet to rescope is the Stop-hook bullet under the section headed `### Supporting cast: journal, hooks, resume`. Its equivalence claim is limited to the carve-out itself — the failed-plus-`pending` reclassification — and must not assert that the hook reproduces every other aspect of I.2.a's unlaunched definition. The bullet remains a citing consumer of I.2.a (which remains the sole normative statement); it must not restate the rule in a form that can drift independently. The I.2.b step 1 citation literal is not edited.
