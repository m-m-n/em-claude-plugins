## Goal

## Design

### The region

The env-route body reading is every lexer call `_args_json_env_payload_values()` makes while reading the body: the calls reached through `_args_json_payload_chunk()`, `_args_json_chunk_statements()`, `head()`, `_args_json_lifted_notion_words()` and `_args_json_read_values()` (SPEC.md FR1). Nothing outside this region changes.

The state the region must leave as it found it:

| State | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `_lex_unreadable_met` | module-level flag: an unreadable heredoc delimiter was met |
| `_lex_extglob_met` | module-level flag: an extended-pattern parenthesis was met |
| `_lex_extglob` | the current extglob reading setting |

### Flow

1. At entry to the region, record the current values of the three states above.
2. First reading: read the body under the current extglob setting, as today, and note whether any lexer result returned during this reading reports `ext_met` as true.
3. When step 2 noted `ext_met`: read the same body a second time under the opposite extglob setting, then put the extglob setting back to its entry value.
4. Combine the readings:
   - the notion values found by either reading are returned and checked;
   - a value both readings return (the decoded command string, a candidate body) is returned once and charged once;
   - overrun and unrestorable are reported when either reading raised them.
5. Put the two flags back to their entry values, after both readings.
6. Steps 3 and 5 hold on every exit from the region: a normal return, every early return, and an exception propagating out (for example `LexBudgetExceeded`). The exception itself propagates unchanged.

When step 2 notes no `ext_met`, only one reading runs and the result is today's, apart from step 5.

### Contract of the region

- **Postcondition, every exit**: `_lex_unreadable_met`, `_lex_extglob_met` and `_lex_extglob` equal their values at entry. The restored value is the entry value, never a fixed false (THREAT-MODEL.md TM-1).
- **Postcondition, normal return**: the returned notion values are the de-duplicated union of the readings; the charge is that of the distinct values; overrun / unrestorable are the logical OR of the readings.
- **Unchanged handling**: an exception leaving the region is handled exactly as today (`_judge_under()` still returns the scan-budget ask).
- **Unchanged downstream**: values found are queued to `statements()` as today; flags raised while `statements()` lexes them reach `run()` as today — they are outside the region.

### Invariants (NFR1, NFR2)

- Lexer calls outside the region, `run()`'s condition for starting the second whole-command reading (`_judge_under(payload, True)`), the extglob-switch judgment and the unreadable-heredoc-delimiter judgment are unchanged.
- The region records no extglob unit: `statements()` stays the only caller of `_note_extglob_units()`.

## Test Notes

- Write the decision cases first and confirm TS-1 and TS-2 fail before changing the hook (red), as `.claude/rules/hook-tests.md` asks for a false positive.
- The variant checks in AC-2 (restore to false) and AC-3 (flag restoration without the second reading) are made by temporarily altering the implementation; the variants are not committed, and their outcome is recorded with the task's test record.
- TS-6's body is built from the existing mcp-bridge-function-shadow notion-pair case in `destructive-guard-cases.json`; confirm with the lexer that the extglob-off reading discards the preceding line and the extglob-on reading shows the notion pair before fixing the case.
- The unit tests load the hook module in-process from its file path (the file name has hyphens). They do not depend on `CLAUDE_BATCH` from the environment.
- To reach the exception exit in AC-4 and the one-sided overrun / unrestorable in AC-5, a test may lower the scan budget or substitute the lexer for the duration of the test; every module-level value a test changes is put back afterwards, because all tests share one process under unittest discovery.
- Cases carry no machine-specific or home-directory path.
