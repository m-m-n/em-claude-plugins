# Task Plan: task0007 — workflow-patch contract for the feature-docs correction

## Goal

workflow-patch.md owns `tasks_patch.feature_docs_removals`: its field, its
permission on the correction entry (where a `failed` task is allowed), its
conditions and their codes, its limits and its application. The existing
permission and verbatim-copy sentences name the new exception.

### Files to Modify

## Design

Work against IMPLEMENTATION.md D7. The feature-docs path predicate is cited
from `skills/plan-writing/SKILL.md`, `## Feature-docs Files`; it is not
restated.

1. **`## \`tasks_patch\``**: after the `path_corrections` paragraph, add a
   paragraph for `tasks_patch.feature_docs_removals`. It is present only on
   a `replace_planning` patch that removes feature-docs paths from an
   existing plan: a non-empty list of `task_id` and `path` mappings, every
   `task_id` in `carried_task_ids`, absent from every other patch, and never
   together with `path_corrections`. It points to the new section.
2. **`### \`replace_all\` permission conditions`**
   - The intro keeps "exactly two paths", `## Final-path correction`,
     `tasks_patch.path_corrections` and "a patch matching none of these is
     rejected". It also names the further state that
     `## Feature-docs correction` admits for a patch carrying
     `tasks_patch.feature_docs_removals`.
   - The protocol-error sentence stays verbatim.
   - The sentence after it keeps naming `## Final-path correction` with
     "correction entry", "`failed` task is not a protocol error" and
     "`in_progress`", and adds `## Feature-docs correction` as having the
     same correction entry.
3. **Re-planning task-id allocation**: the verbatim-copy pointer names both
   exceptions, the final-path correction and the feature-docs correction,
   and keeps the words "exception" and "verbatim".
4. **New H2 `## Feature-docs correction`**, between
   `## Interrupted authorization-spend recovery` and
   `## Final-path correction`. It adds no application rule, and the
   eighteen rules apply as to any patch. It contains:
   - Field and shape (`feature-docs-removal-shape`).
   - A permission-states table:
     - correction entry: admitted; a `failed` task permitted, an
       `in_progress` task rejected; the carry-over and `preserve`
       obligations of the Re-planning path apply;
     - Re-planning path and Initial-planning path:
       `feature-docs-removal-not-permitted`.
   - The conditions with their codes:
     - `-in-flight` and `-merged`, read from `workflow.yaml` and the journal;
     - `-path`;
     - `-empty-files`;
     - `-incomplete`.
   - The limits (`-limits`).
   - The inputs: `--workflow`, `--journal` and `--feature-dir` under
     `--dry-run-apply`. A missing input is an execution error.
   - The application: remove every occurrence of the path from the target's
     `files`, and set `notes` to null. Every other field and every other
     record is copied verbatim.
   - The plan agreement (`task-plan-files-mismatch`).
   - Ownership: implementation-planner corrects the plans and proposes the
     removals; the orchestrator applies the patch. The implementer and
     rework-planner do not.
   - Restoration and ordering are owned by `skills/develop/SKILL.md`,
     `## Step A.6: failed からの入口` (cited).
   - That a `replace_all` without the field behaves as before in every state.
5. **Pins to keep.**
   - In `tests/test_final_path_correction_docs.py`: `## Final-path
     correction` directly before `## Ownership boundary`, and recovery
     before it.
   - In `tests/test_workflow_patch_doc.py` and
     `tests/test_requirements_tests_replace.py`: the rules numbered 1 to 18
     and "All eighteen rules apply".
   - The permission-section phrases pinned by the existing tests.
   - Refresh `WORKFLOW_PATCH_SHA256` per the Conventions.
