# Task Plan: task0001 — SPEC-change transition returns verify to pending

## Goal

The SPEC-change transition's step 2 returns `verify` to `pending` in all three
documents that state the five steps, and the develop skill's 「verify フェーズ」
item 6 cites that, so a verify-sourced SPEC change no longer leaves a stale
`verify: failed` behind it.

## Requirements

FR1, FR6, FR7, FR8, NFR1, NFR2, NFR3, NFR4

## Scope

### Files to Create
- `tests/test_spec_change_verify_pending.py` — recurrence tests for FR7 (a) and for the FR6 citation

### Files to Modify
- `em-workflow/references/rework-task-synthesis.md` — Section 10 only: step 2 of the When rework needs a SPEC.md change block
- `em-workflow/references/contracts/rework-planner-contract.md` — step 2 of the Specification-change transition section
- `em-workflow/agents/rework-planner.md` — the five-step summary in the Specification-change transition section
- `em-workflow/skills/develop/SKILL.md` — 「verify フェーズ」 item 6
- `tests/test_worker_contracts_planning.py` — only if one of its pinned assertions changes; its five-step numbering assertion keeps holding

## Design

Cross-task anchors and conventions: IMPLEMENTATION.md (Shared Components
"SPEC-change transition block"; Conventions). This task owns Section 10 of
`rework-task-synthesis.md`; Section 11 belongs to another task and this task's
diff touches no line of it.

1. **Section 10 (owner).** In the `**When rework needs a SPEC.md change**`
   block, step 2 names `create-plan`, `implement`, `review` and `verify` as
   set to `pending`. Step 2 also states that the transition writes only these
   steps' `status`: `verify`'s `result` and `failed_items` stay until the next
   verify run overwrites them (SPEC A4). The block keeps exactly five numbered
   steps, and the paragraph that opens with "Step 2's `create-plan` re-entry"
   stays directly after them (an existing test slices between the label and
   that paragraph).
2. **Contract.** `rework-planner-contract.md` keeps its five-step list; its
   step 2 names the same four steps.
3. **Agent prompt.** The fixed five-step sequence in `agents/rework-planner.md`
   names `verify` among the steps set to `pending`; the rest of the summary
   ("You trigger this sequence only by returning the question", the five-step
   count) is unchanged.
4. **SKILL.md item 6 (citation only).** One Japanese sentence is added inside
   item 6, after its last existing sentence (the one stating that every
   branch is committed with commit-docs.sh) and before
   「**batch 自動 rework の系譜 cap**」, so it applies to both interactive and
   batch runs. The sentence says that when rework-planner returns `gate_id`
   `rework.spec-change` and the SPEC change is selected, the transition of
   `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/references/rework-task-synthesis.md` Section 10
   returns `verify` to `pending` as well, and it lists none of the
   transition's steps. It does not contain 「文書のみの回」 (an existing test
   locates the docs-only clause by that term). The list under
   「### ターンを終わらせていい唯一の条件」 and Step B are not edited (A8).
5. **Pins that must stay intact**: in SKILL.md the line-broken
   「新しい」 / 「rework task が 1 件以上 workflow.yaml へ登録されるまで戻さない」
   text, the lineage-cap block (byte-identical pin), and the strings
   `tests/test_develop_skill_rewiring.py` pins for the verify section; in
   Section 10 the strings pinned by `tests/test_rework_synthesis_contract.py`
   and `tests/test_spec_change_origin_binding.py`.
