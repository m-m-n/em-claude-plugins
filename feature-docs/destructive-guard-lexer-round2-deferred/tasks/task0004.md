# Task Plan: task0004 — Do not treat `\r` as a blank in the lexer or the word split

## Goal

The lexer's blanks are space and tab only, as in bash 5.3, so a `#` right
after a `\r` is part of a word and no longer opens a comment that hides the
rest of the line (finding aa735aa95be36542). The tokenizer that reads the
masked view splits words the same way, and a `\r` at the end of a word does
not turn a denied destructive command into an allowed one.

## Requirements

FR6, FR9, FR10, FR11, NFR1, NFR4, NFR5, NFR6, NFR7

## Scope

### Files to Create

None.

### Files to Modify
- `em-workflow/hooks/destructive-guard.py` — the blank sets and blank tests of the lexer, the tokenizer's blank set in both of its modes, and the word matching of the decision layer for a trailing carriage return.
- `em-workflow/hooks/tests/destructive-guard-cases.json` — append this task's cases after the last entry.
- `tests/test_destructive_guard_lexer_agreement.py` — a new test class.

## Design

Cross-task rules that apply here (IMPLEMENTATION.md): the character classes
row (blanks are owned by this task; delimiter metacharacters and the line
separator are task0003's), the layer structure (this task is the only one
that touches the decision layer), the internals row (the tokenizer's call
shape is unchanged), the case table append protocol, the test layout row,
lexical pin ownership, the fail-safe direction, the bash confirmation
convention and the existing-cases convention.

### Blanks in the lexer (SPEC FR6)

- Space and tab are the only blanks of the shell-rule frames: they end a
  word, are skipped between words, leave the next character at a word
  start, and end a bound subscript. `\r` is an ordinary word character in
  all of these roles. This covers the blank pattern, the word-run and
  word-end sets, the bound-subscript stop set and the inline blank test
  SPEC.md FR6 lists.
- The delimiter reader's sets are task0003's and stay untouched here; the
  blanks between a here-document operator and its word are already space
  and tab only.

### The tokenizer (SPEC FR6)

- The tokenizer over the masked view splits at space and tab in its layout
  mode, and at space, tab and newline in its plain-word mode; `\r` is never
  a separator in either mode. The verdict path is the same with and without
  `CLAUDE_BATCH`, so the change holds in both hook modes.

### Trailing `\r` in the decision layer (SPEC F3/F6)

- When the decision layer matches a statement's words against its
  destructive rules (command names, options, targets), a `\r` at the end of
  a word is disregarded, so a command text with CRLF line ends keeps the
  verdict it has today. Words are not changed for any other purpose.

### Cases (FR9)

Commands in the case table's JSON string notation (`\n` newline, `\r`
carriage return). Labels begin `aa735aa95be36542 round2-deferred`, then the
tag `FR6.1` to `FR6.4`:

1. deny: `echo x\r# ; rm -rf /home/sakura/valuable`
2. deny: `rm -rf /home/sakura/valuable\r`
3. deny: `git reset --hard\r`
4. allow: `echo hi\r\necho done\r\n`

Cases 2 and 3 keep an existing verdict (SPEC F3/F6) and need no bash
confirmation.

### Not touched

The line index and the delimiter reader (task0003), the tail gate
(task0001), subscript detection (task0002), the command form of `${`
(task0005), and the fallback word split used when the tokenizer fails.

## Acceptance Criteria (MANDATORY)

Commands use the case table's JSON string notation. "Both modes" means
without `CLAUDE_BATCH` and with `CLAUDE_BATCH=1`. Case numbers refer to the
Cases list under Design.

- [ ] AC-1 (FR9): Before any commit that changes `em-workflow/hooks/destructive-guard.py`, a commit appends the four cases of Design → Cases to the case table after its last entry, with the expectations listed there and labels beginning `aa735aa95be36542 round2-deferred FR6.`; the task branch history shows that order.
- [ ] AC-2 (FR6, NFR4, NFR6, TM-6): For case 1, `lex_shell()` reports no comment region; `statements()` yields a statement whose shaped words begin with `rm`; the map is identical after clearing the lexer cache; the decision is deny in both modes.
- [ ] AC-3 (FR6, NFR6, TM-6): `echo a\r#b` has no comment region; `echo a\r #b` has a comment region starting at the `#`; in `echo a\rb` the lexer reads `a\rb` as one word (no blank between `a` and `b`).
- [ ] AC-4 (FR6, TM-6): The tokenizer over the plain masked view of `a\rb c` yields exactly the words `a\rb` and `c`, in its layout mode and in its plain-word mode.
- [ ] AC-5 (NFR6, NFR7, TM-6): Cases 2 and 3 are deny in both modes; case 4 and `echo hi\r` are allow in both modes.
- [ ] AC-6 (NFR1, TM-8): For each of cases 1 to 4 repeated 200 and 400 times (joined by a newline), the work for 400 is at most 2.5 × the work for 200 + 100 and at most `LEX_WORK_FACTOR` × length + 1024.
- [ ] AC-7 (NFR5, NFR7, FR10): No pre-existing case-table entry is removed, edited or reordered and every pre-existing case keeps its verdict; `python3 em-workflow/hooks/tests/run-destructive-guard.py`, `python3 -m unittest tests.test_destructive_guard_lexer_agreement` and `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests` all pass, including `TestPositionMap`, `TestStageAgreement` and `TestModuleContract`.
