# Task Plan: task0012 — routed-arm exit and origin verification in batch question resolution

## Goal

`em-workflow/references/question-resolution.md` states, in one consistent
voice, that a `spec-change` question leaves the Batch resolution sequence at
the routed arm and is resolved only by the Classification gate; that the
three immediate-abort arms are evaluated first and their abort is final; and
that the gate verifies the ORIGINATING review finding's category and
irreversibility from the review record rather than from the worker-set
labels. `references/contracts/rework-planner-contract.md` drops its
superseded "batch aborts a specification change" paragraph and carries the
packet obligation the verification depends on.

## Design

Four defects share one root cause: the revision that gave `spec-change` a
routed arm never propagated to the sequence steps and contract paragraphs
that were written against the previous "spec-change always aborts" fact.

1. **Exit from the Batch resolution sequence.** The sequence's step 2 states
   today only that an aborted question never reaches step 3. Add the second
   outcome: a question the routed arm sends to the Classification gate also
   leaves the sequence at that point — it never reaches the policy lookup,
   never reaches the Unlisted-gate fallback, and never falls through to
   `on_unanswered`. The gate's own proceed/stop outcome and its audit record
   are the resolution for that question.

2. **Precedence reservation on the routed arm.** The routed arm currently
   declares an unconditional departure keyed on `category: spec-change`
   alone. State the reservation: the routed arm applies only when none of the
   three immediate-abort conditions (`category: security`, `category:
   license`, an `assumptions[]` entry naming the question with
   `reversible: false`) holds. When a question satisfies both, the abort arm
   is evaluated first and its abort is final and non-overridable — the routed
   arm never converts an abort into a classification.

3. **Origin verification inside the gate.** `category` and `gate_id` are
   worker-set, so a relabelled packet must not be able to widen its own
   route. Add a verification step ahead of classification: the question names
   the originating review finding(s) by `stable_id` together with the review
   round record that carries them; the orchestrator reads each finding's
   `category` from THAT record, never from the worker-set `category`, and
   aborts when any originating finding's category is `security` or
   `license`, or when an `assumptions[]` entry naming the question carries
   `reversible: false`. An origin that is absent, unresolvable, or does not
   match a finding in the named record aborts as well — fail-closed, so a
   packet with no verifiable origin cannot reach classification. Every one of
   these aborts records its reason and the evidence considered, and none of
   them raises a batch confirmation nobody can answer (NFR3).

4. **Unlisted-gate fallback step 8.** Its premise sentence is now false.
   Rewrite it so the two mechanisms are distinguished: security, licensing
   and irreversible-operation questions never reach the branch because the
   Fail-closed classification ABORTED them; specification-change questions
   never reach it because the routed arm REMOVED them from the sequence at
   step 2. Keep the branch's own rule (a merely preferential choice on a
   success path takes the smallest side effect) and the statement that it
   replaces `references/batch-mode.md`'s continue-on-success-path rule.

5. **Contract alignment.** `rework-planner-contract.md`'s paragraph asserting
   that `rework.spec-change` "falls to the unlisted-gate fallback ... aborts
   rather than proceeding" is superseded. Replace it with the citation-only
   form `references/rework-task-synthesis.md` Section 10 already uses — batch
   resolution goes through the classification gate defined in
   `references/question-resolution.md`, cited and not restated — and add the
   packet obligation from item 3: the packet a rework-planner returns for
   `gate_id: rework.spec-change` names each originating finding's `stable_id`
   and the review round record path in the question's `evidence[]`, because
   the gate's origin verification reads them.
