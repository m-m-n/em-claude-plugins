# Task Plan: task0003 — round-record field definition and batch report item

## Goal

review-phase.md defines the `verify_resolution` field and the fixed-format
`resolution_reason` of a finding fixed by verify pass; batch-mode.md's
Reporting and its audit-item source map show those findings apart from the
ones that stay deferred.

## Requirements

FR5, FR10, NFR1, NFR2, NFR3, NFR5

## Scope

### Files to Create
- `tests/test_verify_resolution_record_docs.py` — text tests for every
  Acceptance Criterion below

### Files to Modify
- `em-workflow/references/review-phase.md` — the round record's finding
  fields gain `verify_resolution` and the fixed-format reason
- `em-workflow/references/batch-mode.md` — Reporting's deferred-findings
  audit item and the audit-item source map

## Design

Contracts used, not restated here: IMPLEMENTATION.md C4 (rewritten finding
shape) and C6 (latest round); D4 (the report reads the round record).

### review-phase.md

In the place that defines the round record's finding fields (`stable_id`,
`severity`, `category`, `resolution`, `resolution_reason`), add:

- `verify_resolution`: optional, present only on a finding fixed by verify
  pass; a mapping with exactly the five C4 keys `verify_round`,
  `verified_commit`, `criteria_ids`, `previous_resolution`,
  `previous_resolution_reason`, each with its type and meaning.
- The `resolution_reason` of such a finding: the C4 sentence
  `resolved by verify round {N}: acceptance criteria {IDs} passed`.
- The writer: `em-workflow/scripts/resolve-verified-findings.py`, during the
  verify phase, on the latest round record only. When and under which
  conditions it runs is cited from `skills/develop/SKILL.md`「verify フェーズ」,
  not restated.
- Such a finding counts as resolved for the merge preflight because its
  `resolution` is `fixed`; the preflight's rule itself is unchanged.

Wording rules (NFR2): R5's Completion gate paragraph stays word for word,
and R5 gains neither `gate_id:` nor `batch-policies.yaml`. Read
`tests/test_review_phase_coverage_record.py` before editing.

### batch-mode.md

- Reporting: the deferred-findings audit item shows two groups. (a) Fixed by
  verify pass: each with its stable_id, its verify round
  (`verify_resolution.verify_round`) and its criteria IDs
  (`verify_resolution.criteria_ids`). (b) Still deferred: listed as today.
  The item carries only those structured values; `resolution_reason`,
  `previous_resolution_reason` and finding text are not transcribed (NFR1).
- Audit-item source map: group (a) comes from the latest review round
  record's findings with `resolution: fixed` and a `verify_resolution`;
  group (b) keeps its existing source, the findings with resolution
  `deferred`.

Wording rule (NFR2): no digit 1 or 3 within 60 characters of
`verify_rework`, 系譜 or `hard cap`. Read
`tests/test_verify_rework_lineage_cap.py` before editing.
