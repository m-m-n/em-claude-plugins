## Design

Decisions D2 to D7 of IMPLEMENTATION.md are the rules the documents state, so the
wording here must agree with them. These documents add no gate, user question or
`workflow.yaml` field.

### workflow-patch.md

- Condition 4 also states the `new_path` form.
- Condition 5 keeps its sentence and adds that on the Re-planning path it requires
  only that no old path and no bare old file name remain.
- The comparison paragraph states FR3, FR5, FR6 and FR7 and the document classes.
  Its markers stay: `**Comparison` and `**Conditions.**` to `**Ownership.**`.
- A new paragraph between the comparison and `**Ownership.**` states the FR1 limits
  and the two new codes. It is outside the five numbered conditions, and no numbered
  item is added between `**Conditions.**` and `**Comparison`.
- FR9 is stated with its exception and its precedence.

These phrases stay and remain true of the positional comparison:

- `IMPLEMENTATION.md, VERIFICATION.md and each target task's plan`
- `the version at the base commit and the current version`
- `a single left-to-right pass`
- `replaces each old path by its new path`
- `each old file name by its new file name`
- `longer match first`
- `The two results must be equal`
- `the current version must not contain an old path`
- `New task plans under `entries` are outside the set`

No sentence may say that both versions are normalized to one text.

`A document absent at the base commit is outside the set` no longer holds on the
correction entry. The text changes and so does its pin in
`test_comparison_set_exclusions`.

### implementation-planner.md

The section keeps its existing phrases and has no sub-heading. It adds:

- the `new_path` form
- that a non-target unmerged plan may have only references replaced, and a merged
  task's plan is not edited
- the canonical plan of a new task, line by line as D6 gives it
- which new name replaces a shared bare name
- what `entries` and `requirements_patch` may hold on the correction dispatch
- that on a re-planning pass the body may change and no old path or bare old file
  name may remain

### create-plan-phase.md

The bullet that lists the correction codes in section 9 adds
`path-correction-requirements` and `path-correction-entries`. It keeps
`path-correction-in-flight`, `path-correction-content` and every other pinned phrase.

### Pin

Refresh the workflow-patch.md byte pin under D7, as the last edit.
